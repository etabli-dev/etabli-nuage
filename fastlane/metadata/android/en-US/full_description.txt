Établi Nuage checks links from a local CSV or a CSV fetched from your Nextcloud server. Choose the URL column and header interpretation, inspect page loads or HTTP status checks, and share completed CSV logs or upload them through WebDAV.

Saved logs remain selectable after restart. Runs are active-screen operations; automatic background scheduling or queue resumption is not implemented. Contacts, calendar and a general file browser are future work.

No analytics or author-operated service. Checks contact the URLs you supply; pages may load external resources. Nextcloud transfers require HTTPS and an app password stored in the platform secure store. Logs contain checked URLs, timestamps and a per-install identifier. Not affiliated with Nextcloud GmbH.
